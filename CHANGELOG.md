# Changelog

All notable changes to **Subject–Predicate–Object Structural Intelligence (SPO-SI)** are documented here.

SPO-SI is an **open research incubator**. Its initial release contains directional hypotheses, conceptual architecture, exploratory relay contracts, and proposed validation procedures. It does **not** claim that the proposed architecture has been experimentally validated.

### Research position
- Defined SPO-SI as a **directional hypothesis and open structural relay initiative**, not a comprehensive theory of world models, consciousness, or artificial general intelligence.
- Emphasized subject-centered and shared world models as potentially complementary architectures, rather than asserting one universally superior approach.
- Distinguished functional agency, self-modeling, self-modification, validated improvement, and phenomenal consciousness.
- Prioritized evidence provenance, counter-evidence, evaluator governance, and collective structural learning.
