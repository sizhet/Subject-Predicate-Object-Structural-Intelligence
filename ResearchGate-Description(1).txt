Subject–Predicate–Object Structural Intelligence (SPO-SI) is an exploratory research framework for organizing structural intelligence around subjects, predicates, objects, world models, governed actions, and evolving relationships. The project examines how subject-centered representations, composable intelligence units, structural relay, and explicit contracts may support interoperable and progressively evolving human–AI systems. This v0.1.0 research release provides conceptual papers, architectural figures, draft relay specifications, and a research and validation agenda. Its proposals are hypotheses and architectural designs, not claims of experimentally demonstrated capabilities. Authors: Sizhe Tan & GPT-Obot.
