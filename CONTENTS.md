## 7. Research Status and Interpretation

SPO-SI is an **open directional hypothesis and architecture research program**. Its subject–predicate–object framing is an organizing proposal, not a claim to have discovered subject–environment interaction for the first time. Related traditions include control theory, POMDPs, embodied cognition, active inference, and multi-agent systems. Claimed advantages of SPO-SI remain to be tested against established baselines.

Functional agency, self-modeling, recursive modification, and subjective consciousness must not be conflated. No claim of machine consciousness follows from the SPO architecture.

External researchers are invited to critique assumptions, supply counter-examples, propose alternative interfaces, connect relevant prior work, and independently test hypotheses. Contributions should state **what is claimed, what evidence supports it, what remains unknown, and what would change the conclusion**.
