## Overview

This initial release introduces **Subject–Predicate–Object Structural Intelligence (SPO-SI)** as a research framework for describing and organizing structural intelligence around subjects, predicates, objects, and their evolving relationships.

The project investigates how subject-centered world models, structural operations, governed actions, interoperable relay mechanisms, and recursive structural evolution may be represented within a coherent architecture. It is intended as a conceptual and specification-oriented research contribution, not as a claim of a fully implemented or experimentally validated intelligence system.

## Research Contributions

- **SPO-oriented structural representation:** A common conceptual vocabulary for relating subjects, predicates, objects, and structural intelligence processes.
- **Subject-centered modeling:** Explicit attention to subject boundaries, perspective, agency, and the relationship between local and shared world models.
- **Structural composition and relay:** Exploration of portable structural units and interoperability across components and intelligence systems.
- **Governed action and evolution:** Architectural directions for controlled structural updates, evaluation, and recursive growth.
- **Testability:** Open research questions and validation directions that distinguish proposals from demonstrated results.

## Scope and Limitations

**v0.1.0 is a research-incubator release.** The architecture, terminology, and proposed interfaces are exploratory. The release does not establish empirical performance gains, interoperability conformance, autonomous recursive self-improvement, or production readiness. These claims require subsequent implementation, benchmarks, and independent validation.
